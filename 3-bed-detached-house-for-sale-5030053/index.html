Bergins are delighted to present Woodmere, a detached three bedroom home of approx. 231 sq m (2,487 sq ft) on Ballinclea Road. It was designed and built in 2014 with comfort, efficiency and flexible living in mind.

Behind a gravel drive with parking for two cars, the house has a clean, contemporary twin gable front with tall windows and a brick band detail. The wide hall runs straight through to the kitchen. The ground floor is laid out so that every main room opens directly onto the garden or a courtyard, and floor to ceiling glazing brings light deep into the house throughout the day.

At the heart of the home is an exceptional kitchen, dining and living room of almost 9m x 8m, with a long rooflight over the kitchen. Sliding doors open to the rear terrace and the large, sunny garden. In the living area, glazed corner doors slide fully open onto a private, walled courtyard paved in Indian sandstone and framed by mature trees, so the inside and outside become one space in good weather.

The layout is adaptable. The front living room has its own ensuite shower room, so it could easily become a ground floor bedroom for guests, extended family or future needs. Upstairs, the main bedroom sits behind the full height glazed gable at the back of the house. It has a double height vaulted ceiling, an ensuite, and its own south facing balcony with a glass balustrade overlooking the garden. There are two further generous double bedrooms, one on the first floor and one on the top floor.

The rear garden is a generous lawn with mature, well-stocked borders and raised planted beds, plus substantial south facing terraces for outdoor dining.

With a B BER, an air to water heat pump, heat exchange ventilation and underfloor heating throughout, Woodmere is as efficient as it is comfortable. Wide plank solid wood flooring (Trunk Castile) runs throughout.

Woodmere is on Ballinclea Road in Killiney, one of South County Dublin's most sought-after residential areas. Killiney Shopping Centre is close by for everyday needs. The area is well served by schools, including St Joseph of Cluny Secondary School, Holy Child Killiney and Loreto Abbey Dalkey. Killiney Hill and Dalkey Hill are nearby, with their well-known coastal walks and views. The villages of Dalkey, Glasthule and Dún Laoghaire are all within easy reach, with their cafés, restaurants and shops. There are good transport links, including Dublin Bus and the DART, and the N11 and M50 give easy access to the city and to Wicklow.
